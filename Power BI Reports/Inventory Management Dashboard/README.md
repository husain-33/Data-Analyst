Insights are drawn to support strategic decision-making in product inventory and sales management.

3.	Sales Performance Overview:
•	Highest sales were recorded in October, followed by July and June.
•	Sales steadily increased mid-year, peaking in Q3.
•	United States leads with 39% of total sales.
•	Germany follows with 29%, then Canada (20%) and the UK (12%).
•	Male buyers contributed to 60% of total sales, compared to 40% from female buyers.
•	Targeting male segments may yield higher sales opportunities.

4.	Detailed Insights:
•	Sizes 8 and 9 show peak sales volumes, especially among male customers.
•	Suggests stocking more products in these sizes for both genders.
•	GER2 and GER1 are the top-performing shops, each nearing 0.3M in sales.
•	US1 to US5 show moderate performance; CAN3 and CAN2 lag behind.
•	Germany has the highest sale price totals and discount sum.
•	Indicates active pricing strategy with volume sales.

5.	Recommendations:
•	Focus marketing campaigns in Germany and the United States.
•	Promote product sizes 8–9, especially for the male segment.
•	Reevaluate performance of lower-performing shops like CAN3.
•	Continue visual enhancements for better stakeholder communication.
